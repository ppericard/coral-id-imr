Privacy

IMR Coral ID, a training tool for coral identification at the Institute for Marine Research (IMR), Dauin, Philippines. Run by Pierre Pericard. Last updated 2 November 2026 (error reports and the one-tap question).

Without signing in

Your progress and settings stay in your browser, on your device (local storage). Your answers in the games (the genus shown, your answer, the choices offered, the photos shown, the game and the time) are also sent, without any name or code, under a random id made on your device, to the Google Sheet described below. Once there are enough of them to show real play (not random clicks), they help rate how hard each photo is and which genera get mixed up, as everyone's do. If you sign in later, they are attached to your name.

If something goes wrong in the app (an error, a photo that does not load, a save that fails), a short technical report is sent to the same Google Sheet: the error message, the page, the app version and the type of browser, under another random id made on your device, never your name. Now and then, after an answer, the app asks whether the questions are too easy, about right or too hard; if you tap an answer, it is sent the same way, with how many questions you have answered and how many of your last 10 were right. You can ignore the question (×).

If you sign in

You sign in with a name of your choice and a 4-digit code. The following is then saved in a Google Sheet owned by Pierre Pericard, through a Google Apps Script:

  • your name, and your code in a scrambled form (a hash): the code itself is not stored;
  • your progress on each genus, and your settings in the app;
  • each answer you give in the games: the genus shown, your answer, the choices offered, the photos shown, the game and the time;
  • your progress (%) and whether you want to be on the leaderboard.

This is used to save your progress across devices, to let IMR trainers follow the training, and to rate how hard each photo is. The photo ratings and the game statistics use anonymous ids, not names; apart from the leaderboard below, only the app's administrators see names.

Leaderboard

If you sign in, your name, your progress (%) and your Elo score (your level, computed from your answers) appear on the public leaderboard of the app (🏆), where anyone using the app can see them. You can leave it at any time: ⚙ Settings, Leaderboard: Off. Nothing else about you is shown there.

Problem reports and feedback

When you report a problem or send feedback, the report, your note and your name (if you give it or are signed in) are saved in the same Google Sheet, so the photos and texts can be fixed.

What is not collected

No advertising, no analytics or tracking scripts, no cookies, no e-mail address, no location. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Other services the pages load from

Like any website, these see your device's IP address when they serve a file: GitHub Pages (the app itself and its fonts), iNaturalist (some coral photos) and Google (the sync script, when you are signed in or send a report).

Deleting your data

Sign out in ⚙ to clear the app from your device. To have your saved name, progress and answers deleted from the Sheet, ask Pierre Pericard at IMR, or open an issue at github.com/ppericard/coral-id-imr.
