What makes a 5

REE-275 question (b) and REE-8. 46 photos in play whose recorded tags all read the reference look: 31 the judge typed 4, 15 it typed 5. Fish and sea slugs; corals have no ease, so none here.

For each photo: the criteria below, each marked ✓ or ✗ with a few words, the grade the marks give (5 with no ✗, one step less per ✗) and the grade the judge typed. Say whether you agree, line by line or for the whole photo. Nothing to type unless you disagree. Where the two grades differ (yellow), your answer is the point.

The criteria of a 5 (the same for every photo)

    Marked from the recorded tags (tag) or by an agent looking at the photo (eye); C3 and C8 are not in the tags, so they are always by eye. Not criteria (settled): juvenile, sex or colour phase, watermark (Q373, Q435, rubric 3).

    How to answer

    Tap All marks right → N when every line is right and the photo deserves the grade the marks give (N), whatever the judge typed. Otherwise tap ✗ on a line you disagree with and add one word; give your own grade if it differs. Then Copy answers (bottom) and paste them in the chat. Your taps are kept in this browser.

    Or dictate by number: 12 ok · 12 C1 non, il est de profil · 12 note 5, fond.
