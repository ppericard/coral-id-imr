The live mode (Pierre, 2026-11-17, Q393: a trainer and a group of students answer the same photo questions at the same time, through this script, polled; docs/reviews/2026-11-18-live-mode-design.md): `POST {lv: 'start' | 'host' | 'join' | 'poll' | 'answer'}` (`lv_`); a session is started only by an account holding the trainer role (its name and code in `lname` / `lpin`, checked against the RAs tab, then `can_(…, 'live', 'instance:imr')`: the right `live`, now the `trainer` role's; one Audit row a start, refusals capped); the trainer's page sends each question it builds with the game's engine, the script keeps its right answer until the reveal and scores each student's answer (a session only in a subject open to every player, never one in beta for a group), 100 points plus up to 100 for the time left, timed from the moment the question first reached that student; sessions, rosters and answers live in the script cache only (6 hours at most, never a sheet tab); joins capped at 40 a session and 400 every 6 hours. Signed-in students' answers are logged by their own app through the usual save, mode `live`. Each student's poll (`hx`: how many revealed questions their page went through) brings their own accepted answers to the questions revealed since (`past`: right choice, accepted choice, choices, photos), so their app logs every accepted answer, and only those, even one whose reveal it missed.
