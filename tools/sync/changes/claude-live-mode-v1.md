The live mode (Pierre, 2026-11-17, Q393: a trainer and a group of students answer the same photo questions at the same time, through this script, polled; docs/reviews/2026-11-18-live-mode-design.md): `POST {lv: 'start' | 'host' | 'join' | 'poll' | 'answer'}` (`lv_`); a session is started only by an account holding the trainer role (its name and code in `lname` / `lpin`, checked against the RAs tab, then `can_(…, 'live', 'instance:imr')`: the right `live`, now the `trainer` role's; one Audit row a start, refusals capped); the trainer's page sends each question it builds with the game's engine, the script keeps its right answer until the reveal and scores each student's answer, 100 points plus up to 100 for the time left, timed from the moment the question first reached that student; sessions, rosters and answers live in the script cache only (6 hours at most, never a sheet tab); joins capped at 40 a session and 400 every 6 hours. Signed-in students' answers are logged by their own app through the usual save, mode `live`.
