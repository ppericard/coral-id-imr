Every leaderboard follows the rules: a player whose saved copy applied an older rules version of a subject (corals `RVS`, fish `FISH_RVS`, any subject's `rvsOf`) is ranked on that subject's board by the progress and level their app will hold once it adopts the replay of their log (core.js `replayAdopted`, the record `sync.js` `replayAdoptS` adopts: `replayedOf` over the saved copy, with the floor of a subject replayed with one), from the record its Replayed tab keeps for today's rules, row count and reset (`boardRpl_`); a player up to date, or one with no record yet, by the saved copy as before; the board only reads (nothing fetched, no lock) and stays cached 5 minutes. The 15-minute trigger, once up to date, makes those records ahead for every listed player behind the rules (`boardWarm_`: the replay of `replay_`, `rplMake_`, outside the script lock, written under a short lock unless the row changed, at most `BOARD_WARM_MS` of new replays a run), the same record the player's next sync reuses, drops the boards that changed from the cache and counts them in the Deploy log (Pierre, 2026-11-09, rule D8: "whenever progress is recalculated, the leaderboard must be recalculated automatically, even if people do not reconnect").
