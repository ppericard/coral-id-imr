One audited write for every rights change, groups by scope, and the guards that keep an admin and a manager (REE-31, step 1 of `docs/decisions/2026-11-26-roles-groups-assignments.md`; Q408, Pierre, 2026-11-19; his words of 2026-11-20 on the Reports tab, «Groups have to be either instance wide or subject specific»; Q447, 2026-11-21 05:40; his answers of 2026-11-26 ~09:50–09:55, points 2, 3, 4 and 7, each «ok»; step 1 only, 2026-11-27 ~08:40, «ok»; `docs/decisions/2026-11-21-groups-by-scope.md`). `rightsWrite_` is the only code that writes the admin list and the standbys (the Roles tab), a group's Rights cell (`gdefPut_` writes the settings only) and the memberships of the groups that carry rights (`groupSet_` refuses such a group): the admin list's grant and revoke, a standby named or not, a join and a leave, a group's rights given or taken (`groupMake`, `groupEdit`, and `groupDrop`, which takes a group's rights away first and asks the grant right for a group that carries them), Pierre's seed, the rights migration, and an account's delete, merge or archive (`rightsGone_`, first, so a stop or a failure changes nothing of the account). One write: the change is simulated, written, then its Audit rows in one write of the tab; rows that cannot be written take the change back and the request answers an error (`server`), its retry makes it. The Audit tab gains two columns at its end, Before and After (old rows blank): each account whose rights change has a row with its whole rights before and after (`grant`/`revoke admin`, `join`/`leave <group id>`, `rights (via <group id>)`), a group's rights a row `rights <group id>` (its rights before and after, the mark `rightsAudited_` reads with the older `right+`/`right-` rows); the row type `rights <before> > <after>` is gone. Rights rows name an account by its audit id (`auid_`: `u:` and a keyed hash of the account's generation `_acct`, one per account lifetime, never derived from its name), as the admin's other rows about an account do; `roles` lists it as `au`. A right in the Rights column is `right@instance:imr` or, for `content`, `live` and `cohorts` only, `right@subject:<id>`; a group's rights are at the instance or at a list of subjects, each right at each of them (`groupScope_`; otherwise `scope`); any other scope is refused (`input`), and an entry written by hand that the script does not take gives nothing and is a Deploy log line. `can_` reads a right held at a subject at that subject only, a right held at the instance at each of its subjects; a live game asks `live` at its subject, and each later question and reveal asks it again (`lvHost_`: a right taken since, or a code changed, ends the game and answers `role`; a game started before this version goes on). The guard (`seatsOf_`): no change takes the instance's admins (live accounts on the admin list under their code) or managers (live accounts holding players, settings and grant at the instance, the admin list's grant counting) from one or more to none; it stops, writes only its row (Result `last`, Before and After the counts) and answers `{error: 'last', seat}`. Sent again with `heir` (an account's name), it answers `confirm` with a confirmation bound to the caller, the request, the script's version and the rights state, valid 5 minutes (`confirmOk_`), and with it hands the seat over in the same write (`(handover)` rows; `heir` when the seat would still be empty). A player's own delete is never stopped: a standby of the seat (`roleGrant {role: 'standby', scope: 'platform' | 'instance:imr'}`, a Roles tab row holding no right) takes it over (`(standby)` rows); with none, the seat stays empty, said by a `seat <seat> empty (self)` row (Result `vacant`) and a Deploy log line. The grant right is given to a group only through a confirmed request of its own (`confirm`, with `members`: how many would hold it; with other changes, `separate`). No other column or tab changes; the app reads no right and nothing reads Before and After, so no gate. The admin page asks before giving Give rights and words `last`, `scope`, `separate` and the one-write `server`.
