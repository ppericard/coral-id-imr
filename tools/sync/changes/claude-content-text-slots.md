The embedded core.js holds the content editor's text slots (Pierre, 2026-11-15, Q359 and Q366; docs/reviews/2026-11-14-sheet-overlay-design.md v2, step 1): each subject's slot lists (`SUBJECTS` `content`: the fish families' tips, phases, look-alike lines, marks, life, juveniles note and a free note; the genera's tips and note; the species' note) and the one function that lays the edits stored in the instance's curation (`curation.json` `content` in each subject's part, `{nodes: {node id: {slot: {v, was, src, t, by}}}}`) over the built file (`contentApply`), with their states (fresh, stale, absorbed) and what is never applied (orphaned). The script does not read the curation for its replay: text edits never move progress, and the structure slots (circles and pairs) come with their own step.
