Acropora	Axial corallite at branch tips | Cup-shaped radial corallites
Pocillopora	Branches covered in verrucae (warts) | Corallites immersed on the verrucae
Stylophora	Blunt branch tips | Hooded corallites (facing down) | Smooth skeleton from afar
Anacropora	No axial corallite | Corallites look empty; smooth surface
Seriatopora	Thin, tangled branches | Corallites in neat rows | No hooded corallites
Porites	Small corallites, shared walls | Honeycomb pattern
Tubastraea	Dark green to black, or bright orange | Round tubular corallite at each branch end
Echinopora	Untidy branching tangles | Whole colony made of small beads | Spines between large corallites
Astreopora	Massive | Cylindrical corallites standing out | Corallites look like holes
Galaxea	Spiky, star-like corallites | Blade-like septa
Turbinaria	Thin plates, regular colony | Smooth coenosteum between corallites | Corallites 2-3 mm
Mycedium	Large nose-shaped corallites | Facing out towards the colony edge
Lobophyllia	Large, fleshy corallites that look isolated | Large spiky septal teeth
Pavona	Smooth, star-like septo-costae between corallites | Corallites on both sides (club-like or leafy)
Pachyseris	Valleys with parallel ridges | Finely ribbed septa | Leafy, laminar growth
Pectinia	Towering small plates of equal height | Maze of hedge-like walls
Platygyra	Brain coral | Septa alternate in size or position (ragged) | Smooth valleys, no paliform lobes
Leptoria	Equal, zipper-like septa | Valleys so narrow you see only the ridges
Goniopora	Long polyps out by day | 24 tentacles (Alveopora: 12) | Tentacles pulse
Plerogyra	Bubble coral: grape-like vesicles
Montipora	Tiniest corallites, look empty | Bumps on the coenosteum are NOT corallites
Hydnophora	Coenosteum covered in conical mounds (monticules) | Corallites in the valleys between them
Fungia	Round free-living disc | Saw-like septal teeth | Pale tentacular lobes
Lithophyllon	Round disc, flat or weakly domed | Fine regular septal teeth: smooth look
Danafungia	Round disc with a central dome or arch | Large septal teeth
Ctenactis	Oval disc with a long mouth furrow | Large saw-like septal teeth
Lobactis	Oval disc | Tall tentacular lobes, often green, blue or white | Regular septal teeth
Herpolitha	Elongated disc, furrow along its whole length | Free-living like a mushroom coral, but many mouths along the furrow | Can form X or Y shapes
Heliofungia	Long anemone-like tentacles by day | White or pink tentacle tips
Dipsastraea	Separate walls, oval corallites | Intra-tentacular budding | Colony not crowded
Astrea	Separate walls, round crowded corallites | Extra-tentacular budding | Septa alternate in size
Diploastrea	Polygonal, thick-walled corallites bunched together
Goniastrea	Paliform lobes | Shared walls, sub-angular corallites | Wall often looks bleached
Favites	Shared or separate walls; crowded, sub-rounded corallites | Extra-tentacular budding | Uneven colony surface
Duncanopsammia	Like Turbinaria, but corallites about 6 mm | Thicker plates, less regular colony
Alveopora	12 tentacles (Goniopora: 24) | Less dense skeleton than Goniopora
