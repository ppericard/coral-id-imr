# Design system (short)

The visual rules every page follows. They came out of the expert UI review of 2026-11-01 (approved by Pierre). Use these tokens and components; do not add one-off sizes, colours or control styles. Tokens live in `:root` at the top of `app.css`.

## Type

- Two families: **Barlow Condensed** (`--display`, headings, big labels) and **Source Sans 3** (`--body`). Both are loaded with their real italics (genus names are always italic): never rely on browser-faked italics or bold.
- Sizes come only from the scale: `--fs-2xs` 11 px (only a glyph inside a small badge), `--fs-xs` 12 px (floor for any readable text), `--fs-s` 14, `--fs-m` 16 (body), `--fs-l` 20, `--fs-xl` 24, `--fs-2xl` 36. SVG schematics may use px inside their viewBox. The one exception is the header logotype (28 px from tablet up, 20 px on phones). A `<small>` is 12 px unless its rule says otherwise. No fluid (`vw`/`clamp`) or `em` font sizes: where text must grow with the screen, take one scale step per size class (e.g. the names on the quiz mix-up photos: 20 / 24 / 36 px).

## Shape

- Radius: `--r-xs` 4 px (keys, small marks), `--r-sm` 6 px (tiles, small cards), `--r` 10 px (cards, panels, photos), `--pill` (buttons, chips, segmented controls).
- Elevation: `--shadow` (resting card), `--shadow-hover` (hover, popovers, pinned buttons). Rings (`inset 0 0 0 Npx`) mark selection, not elevation.

## Colour

- Surfaces and text: `--bg`, `--surface`, `--surface-2`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--line`; brand: `--accent` / `--accent-ink`; states: `--good`, `--bad`, `--warn` (+ `-bg`). Each palette (`:root[data-palette]`) and the dark theme redefine them; the colour-blind mode (`[data-cvd]`) redefines `--good` / `--bad`. `--cf-ink` is the "Coral Finder" source tag (light and dark). `--review` (amber, light and dark) is the "to review" dot of My progress, always on a `--surface` ring (2026-11-03: it replaced a red border and "!"). Text on a `--good` / `--bad` fill is `--surface` (white in light, dark in dark), never a fixed white.
- **No colour literals outside the token blocks** at the top of `app.css` (`:root`, dark theme, palettes, colour-blind). Over photos, which look the same in both themes: `--photo-bg` (black stage and lightbox behind a photo), `--on-photo` (white text and icons on a photo) and `--scrim` (the translucent dark pill behind them); lighter or darker variants are `color-mix()` of these.
- **One meaning per hue on a screen.** Strong colour belongs to the learning-level scale (`--lv1`…`--lv5`, white or ink labels at ≥ 4.5:1). IMR lecture groups use muted tints of their hue with dark text (the number and title carry the group). Coral Finder boxes keep their pale tile colours.
- Right/wrong never by colour alone: tick/cross, and in colour-blind mode a pale fill with a thick solid border (right) vs dashed (wrong); never a solid dark fill, which reads as a pressed button.
- In feedback only "✓ Yes," / "✗ No," takes the right/wrong colour; the genus name to remember stays in ink.

## Lab drawings

- Concept drawings (Corallite anatomy and other schematics) use ONE vivid highlight colour, `--hl` in `proto/lab.css` (raspberry in light, pink in dark), only for the part being explained; everything else fades to grey (`--dk`). Rings on photos use the same colour and dim the rest of the photo (Pierre, PR #177), lightly in Coral basics (2026-11-03: a beginner must still see the subject). The one exception is a drawing that teaches two parts against each other: the second takes `--hl2` (the palette accent), with a key, and the photo's labels carry a dot of the same colour (Coral basics step 4: septa `--hl` inside the rim, costae `--hl2` outside it). `--hl` and `--hl2` are also set on `#lightbox`, for labels shown in the enlarged view.
- Growth-form icons (Coral basics step 7, `FORM_ICON` in `proto/basics.js`): 64 × 48 silhouettes on a seabed line, round strokes in `currentColor` with a soft tint fill, the picked one in the accent.
- A teaching photo that enlarges is itself the button, with the `ZOOMI` mark in its corner, never a separate small "Enlarge" link; the enlarged view can carry the photo's labels (`zoom(list, i, {over})`, a Labels switch).
- Home door schematics are drawn SVG (`home-art.js`), not screenshots; in dark mode the drawings keep the light palette.

## Icons

One SVG set (24 px grid, 1.75 stroke, `currentColor`): see `LAB_ICONS` in `proto/lab.js` for the Tools hub and the inline SVGs in `index.html`. No Unicode pictographs as icons (✺ ⌘ ◐…); plain symbols in text (✓ ✗ ★ ←) are fine. Icons drawn at 18 px (header buttons, the enlarge mark) take a 2 stroke to stay legible.

- **Enlarge mark** (`ZOOMI` in `app.js`, `.zic` in `app.css`; Pierre, 2026-11-03, after the answer log showed players almost never enlarged a quiz photo): a magnifier with a plus (the Identify tab's magnifier + "+", 18 px) in a 32 px `--scrim` circle, `--on-photo` stroke, 8 px from the bottom-right corner of every photo that opens the enlarged view in the quiz, the duels (also on the mix-up montage from tablets up) and the genus sheet (cover and view rows). Always shown, never animated; decorative (`aria-hidden`): the photo's button carries the label, "Enlarge photo" (on the sheet "Enlarge photo: *genus*, *view*"). It replaces the old ⤢ glyph. The source pill (`.psrc`) sits bottom-left and stops short of it.

## Screens

- Phone tab bar: icon above label (same SVG set), the active tab gets the accent colour and a tinted pill behind its icon.
- Header progress pill: "Progress" + a mini ring (`--pp`) + %, nothing else (no status dot: sign-in and saving trouble are the "Sign in" / "Not saved" pill beside it).
- Signing in: while signed out, a filled "Sign in" pill (`.next.signb`) sits in the header on every page and opens the sign-in card; once signed in it disappears and the account is one quiet line at the top of ⚙ (Send now, Sign out), with "Backup or reset progress" folded at the bottom of ⚙. If saving fails, the header pill comes back as an outlined red "Not saved" that opens ⚙. Never put the account on a content page. Entering an existing account asks "Is it you?" in the same box: Yes (`.next`) and No (`.next.sec`) share a row, or each takes a full row when they wrap (`.sconfirm`); the form's message sits on its own line in body colour above the help (`.sform [role=status]`).
- My progress: one card joins the overall ring and the player's badge, compact on phones (about 64 px high) and roomier beside the board on desktop (120 px ring; in the Slate view, which needs only about 680 px, the side column takes the rest of the width and the card puts a 140 px ring beside the text), and comes first (above the board on phones and tablets). Then the board, Slate by default, the level counts, Review first, Often mixed up, and the Slate / Photos / Groups switch at the bottom (Pierre, 2026-11-02: the switch took space at the top and hid the badge under the 39 tiles). No "Practise now" button (the nav already has Practice).
- Quiz on phone/tablet: the stage takes all the height the question and answers leave (Pierre prefers the biggest photo to a tight 4:3 box), sized before the photos load: **nothing may move while a photo loads**. A photo close to that shape fills it (cover) unless that would enlarge it more than 1.15x (`LOWRES`: a few IMR lecture photos are ~300 px); a very different shape, or a small photo, is shown whole on its blurred background, never blown up. Look-alikes does the same (`fitLow()`); genus sheet tiles always fill their box. Full frame in the lightbox; a click there shows the photo at actual size, centred on the point clicked, and a mouse then drags it around (hand cursor; touch screens pan natively); a click without moving goes back to fit (an RA, 2026-11-02).
- Quiz on desktop: the grid of views (side by side or stacked) is picked from each photo's known shape (data.json `ar`) to show the most photo; the stage keeps its full height (no photo above an empty half screen) and is laid out before the photos load. After an answer, Next spans the side column and is pinned to its bottom, level with the bottom of the photos, so it never moves from one question to the next; a long feedback scrolls under it.
- Photo files: `img/<f>.webp` is at most 1600 px, `-m` 960 px, `-s` 480 px and `-t` 240 px on the long side; big photos take the smallest file that covers their box (`fit` in `app.js`, Photo quality setting), thumbnails the 240 or 480 px file (`thumb()`). Grids that crop photos to 4:3 (cards, sheet strips, pair cells) declare in `srcset` the width a 4:3 crop really gets from each file (`coverW()` in `app.js`), so a panorama or portrait photo loads the big file instead of a blown-up thumbnail.
- Learn > Look-alikes: one pair per screen; move with the big round arrows on the photos, a sideways swipe anywhere on the pair, or the keyboard arrows. A one-time "Swipe for the next pair" hint shows on touch screens until the first swipe.
- Quiz enlarge hint (`.zhint`, `quiz.js` `zoomHint`): once per device, a small coach mark over the first photo of an early question, "Tap a photo to see it bigger" ("Click" with a mouse): a `--surface` bubble with `--ink` 14 px semibold text, `--r-sm` corners and `--shadow-hover`, its caret pointing down at the enlarge mark. It fades in (no motion with reduced motion), lets every tap through and goes at the first tap or key, or after 6 s. Never with the tour, a card or an achievement toast.
- Memory on phones and tablets: the controls fit one compact row above the grid (Easy/Medium/Hard, Random/Look-alikes, ↻ new game) and the status one short line, so the grid gets the screen. The grid turns to the screen (more columns on a landscape screen, more rows on a portrait one). Desktop keeps the side column.
- Games wider than the page (`.bleed`, ≥ 1340 px) start on the header's left edge and only grow to the right.
- ID tips are the key content: never clamp or truncate them on a card; they are boxed (`.tipbox`) wherever they appear (sheet, quiz feedback, pair page).
- The ? is on every page: the page's explanations (if any), then one last line, About & credits · Privacy · Send feedback (`helpEnd()`, 2026-11-03).
- **Footer and credits** (Pierre, 2026-11-03, as learning and ID apps do: iNaturalist, Seek, Merlin, Duolingo keep credits, licences and privacy on an About screen reached from a menu, not on every screen): no page footer. Home ends with one quiet centred line, "By Pierre Pericard · for IMR · Credits" (`.hcred`, 12 px muted, inside the one screen), and nothing else carries a credits block. The details live on About and credits (`#about`, `.about`: one card per topic, text at a 68ch measure), linked from that line and from the ? help. Photo credits stay on each photo (rule 11). The Coral Finder book is never credited on Home or in the credits; About may cite it under References only.
- **Home hierarchy** (Pierre, 2026-11-03): Learn & practise is the primary door, Identify the secondary one (Identify serves mostly IMR RAs doing data analysis; every player learns and practises). Phones: the Learn and Practice buttons carry the door (Pierre, second look: the photos are an illustration, they do nothing): full-width rows with the tab bar's icon, label and one-line purpose, taking the height left past the photos; the corallite close-ups are one row of 4 tiles, two rows once the band is 150 px tall, at most 28% of the screen; the door's description shows from tablets up. Identify is one compact row (a small ReefCloud photo beside its one-line kicker, title and button; its description from tablets up). Tablets and desktops: side by side, Learn in the wider column (3 : 2). Practice is the page's one filled button; Open the Coral Finder is outlined.
- Home fits one screen at every size (360 × 640 and up, measured in `tests/pages.smoke.js`): the doors grow to fill the screen above the phone tab bar, the extra height going first to the Learn photos (84 px up to 28% of the screen), then to the Learn and Practice buttons; the kicker lines drop their second half on phones (`.wide`). A smaller screen (320 × 568) scrolls rather than squeezing.
- Header on narrow phones (under 380 px): while the Sign in / Not saved pill shows, the logotype text steps aside (visually hidden, still the home link's name); the logo and every control stay. The only width query outside the three size classes, for the header only.
- Hover looks on choice buttons (quiz answers, Lab game choices, Memory tiles, Coral Finder options) live under `@media (hover: hover)`: on touch screens `:hover` sticks after a tap and the next answer under the finger looked chosen.
- A card that greets the player (the apology card of 2026-11-04, `.unlock.sorry`: kicker "Sorry", button "Thanks") uses their first name only, `core.js` `firstName()` (the first word of the sign-in name, capitalised), else "Hi!" (AGENTS.md rule 20e).
- One overlay at a time: an achievement pop-up waits while a card (`.unlock`) or the tour is open; a card closes when its page is left. Every overlay closes with Escape (cards, ⚙, ? help, enlarged photo, tour). A card keeps the focus inside while open and gives it back on close (`cardShow`).
- Custom quiz selection (`#quiz-custom`, 2026-11-03): every genus in view as a small photo tile with its name (`.cqtile`, a button with `aria-pressed`; picked = the accent ring and a ✓ badge), in IMR lecture groups (default), My mix-ups clusters (italic genus names as the title, the count under it) or Progress groups (`.seg` switch, full width under the search on phones), never A–Z or by growth form; search and quick-pick chips above; the count (the genera picked named under it), Clear all (`.linkish`) and Start (`.next`) in a bar that stays above the phone tab bar (`.cqbar`). `.narrow` is the phone-only counterpart of `.wide` (short tab names: "Custom", "Duels").
- Memory card backs are quiet (pale accent tint + faint logo mark), never a loud pattern.
- Game session trail (quiz and duels, next to the question): 10 small cells for the last 10 answers of this visit, grey until answered, then a full `--good` cell for right and a low `--bad` bar for wrong (shape + colour, readable without colour). Fixed width, never moves the layout. No score text in games (Pierre, 2026-11-01).
