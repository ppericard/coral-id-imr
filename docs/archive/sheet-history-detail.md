**v17** (2026-11-02, self-deploys; Pierre: anonymous players and bots should not rate the photos, players should be encouraged to sign in): photo difficulty and confusions (`stats_`, cache key `stats10`) count only trusted players (`trusted_`: not `~anon`, `TRUST.N` = 10 answers or more, right rate clearly above chance, one-sided z ≥ `TRUST.Z` = 1.645 against each question's guess rate; judged on the whole history at each recompute, so a beginner who gets out of chance counts from then on with all their answers). Every player starts as a beginner, level `PLAYER0` = -1 (about 45% right on an average photo with 4 choices; Pierre: the RAs play from the first day of their course, and players without the IMR course know even less), not as an average player (0), whose misses made the photos look harder. Each answer moves the photos by a weight n / (n + `PHOTO_W.N` = 30) after n answers of the player (Glicko's idea, Glickman 1999, as in Park EDM 2021 and Pelánek 2016's separate uncertainty for students and items: an uncertain level moves the items little, so a beginner's random first answers weigh little), and repeats count against the player's own history with the photo (Duolingo's half-life regression, Settles & Meeder 2016, fits each word's difficulty beside the learner's seen/right/wrong counts with it; PFA): a photo new to the player is expected `PHOTO_W.NEW` = 0.5 harder, one already right twice `PHOTO_W.KNOWN` = 1 easier, the app's own rule (`answer()` `bk`). The photo's answer and right counts are weighed the same way (`p[2] += w`): they damp later steps and are the app's confidence in the rating (`photoDiff`), so zero-weight answers change nothing. A corallites hint left closed is not logged with the answer (not seen when answering), so it is neither rated nor counted as recognised. A first-answer-only rule (Papoušek, Pelánek & Stanislav, EDM 2014, for prior knowledge of geography) was tried and dropped (Pierre): players here learn the corals in the app, so the first answer is the noisiest. Every answer still moves the player's own level (`ras`), from `PLAYER0`. Anonymous answers are kept and count once the device signs in (moved to the name, v12). Names are matched case-blind (`lc_`). `readAnswers` rows carry an anonymous flag (index 11) so `core.js` `trustOf` / `eloOf` / `confusionOf` replay the same rule in the admin (and the admin demo). The sign-in card says the answers of players not signed in don't rate the photos. Admin `{anonDelete: [ids]}` (token) deletes every answer of chosen anonymous ids among the rows without a name (`~anon`, `~deleted`), never a named player's: Players > Not signed in, tick, Delete (test runs; Pierre: no developer submissions in the data).
