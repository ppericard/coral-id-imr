## Questions for an IMR trainer

Never settle these in the app; keep the lecture's version.

- Slide 16 right half: Pierre ~90% sure it is Stylophora (captioned "likely").
- Lecture doesn't cover Merulina, Cyphastrea, Halomitra (starred genera): no lecture group of their own.
- Tubastraea: the lecture says "dark green to black" while most game photos show bright orange colonies (beginner RA review, 2026-11-02). Pierre: two main species, black sun coral (dark green) and sun coral (bright orange); the app's ID tip now gives both (tools/id_tips.txt), the lecture text stays verbatim.
- Plerogyra family: lecture says Euphylliidae; WoRMS lists Plerogyridae (shown as an "Other sources" note).
- Duncanopsammia at Dauin = D. peltata plates; Favites with shared + separate walls.
- Corallite arrangement placements to check: Mycedium, Diploastrea, Pectinia (Corallite anatomy tool).
- Fish (Pierre, 2026-11-04): the IMR fish presentation's slide 4 shows two dorsal fins on a clownfish, while slide 7 says damselfish have a continuous dorsal fin: a slip, or an exception to the rule?
- Fish (Pierre, 2026-11-04): the sweetlips juvenile wording, "swim erratically", against the sources' head-down wriggling swimming (FISH_PATH "Juveniles and colour phases").
- **Favites and its neighbours: for Pierre to raise with IMR** (research report of 2026-11-04; only species present in the Philippines by Pierre's rule, AGENTS.md rule 6e; the app keeps the lecture's wording and adds "Other sources" notes, `tools/apply_favites.py`):
  - Slide 61 notes: Favites "exclusively extra-tentacular". Huang et al. 2014 (WoRMS diagnosis) give Favites intra- and extra-tentacular budding, Dipsastraea intra only; possible wording: "extra-tentacular budding rules out Dipsastraea; Favites can also bud intra-tentacularly".
  - Slide 62: "refer to Astrea / Phymastrea". Corals of the World's Phymastrea pages (colemani, valenciennesi, magnistellata) are Favites species in WoRMS; Astrea's are under Astrea.
  - Paliform lobes as the Goniastrea / Favites separator (slide 40 notes, Goniastrea's ID tip): a well-developed crown also appears in F. pentagona and F. colemani, often in F. vasta (Corals of the World), and in Paragoniastrea russelli and Paramontastraea salebrosa, all recorded in the Philippines.
  - Goniastrea "shared walls": G. stelligera (recorded in the Philippines) has separate-walled, conical corallites.
  - Favites "submassive rather than massive" (low priority): F. vasta and F. magnistellata are massive, often over 1 m (Corals of the World).
  - Dipsastraea "colony not crowded" (low priority): depends on depth, e.g. D. pallida, "closely compacted in shallow water, more widely spaced in deeper water" (Corals of the World).
  - Family spelling: the slate's "Diploastreidae" is "Diploastraeidae" in WoRMS (the app now shows both, the IMR one first).
  - How should RAs label Paragoniastrea, Coelastrea and Paramontastraea in ReefCloud (recorded in the Philippines: P. russelli, C. aspera, P. salebrosa)?
