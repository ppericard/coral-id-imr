| **Analyses: what the multiple-testing correction leaves open** (statistical methods study of 2026-11-05, §5.3; the correction: spec/analyses-method.md, Multiple testing): five open items. (a) A spread (SD) near 0 reads clear because its interval is not valid at the boundary: test variance components by a likelihood-ratio test against a 50:50 mixture of χ²₀ and χ²₁ (Self & Liang 1987, Stram & Lee 1994) or a parametric bootstrap.
