# The privacy page: a short notice, the points the law asks for, no internal detail

Date: 2026-11-19 (Manila). PR: #1095. Rules or docs touched: `privacy.html`.

## Context

The privacy page was rebuilt to say everything the app keeps, down to the anonymous id's salt, the hand-written Notes
lines, the load reports' fields and every file host. Asked to read it, Pierre found it far too long and too detailed.

## Decision

Pierre, 2026-11-19 10:56 Manila: «Arrête d'être plus catholique que le pape... C'est des trucs internes ça...»; «A un
moment on ne peut pas être plus transparent que toutes ces grosses boites qui utilisent toutes nos données....»; «ok»
to what stays after a deletion; 10:58, on a half-page notice covering what the Philippine Data Privacy Act and
California's CalOPPA ask (what is kept, why, who sees it, where and how long, deletion and the player's rights, a
contact): «ok, fais la version courte». The page says those points in plain words and nothing internal.

## Consequences

A change to what the app keeps updates the page only when one of those points changes. The wording is the
integrator's call, easy to correct.
