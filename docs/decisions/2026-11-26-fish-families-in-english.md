# Fish families are named in English, even in the French chat

Date: 2026-11-26. PR: #1617. Rules or docs touched: docs/rules/ship.md W1, docs/rules/README.md, AGENTS.md "Working
with Pierre".

## Context

The integrator, writing to Pierre in French, translated three fish families into French common names ("castagnoles,
grondeurs ; archers").

## Decision

Pierre, 2026-11-26 13:25: «Les familles de poissons il faut vraiment que tu prennes l'habitude de les nommer en anglais,
parce que c'est comme ça que je les connais. Mon niveau de noms communs en français est malheureusement limité ^^». On
where to record it, 13:26: «Dans les règles».

- In the French chat, fish families keep their English common names (Sea chubs, Grunters, Archerfishes), never a French
  translation.

## Consequences

Rule W1 carries it, with a few words in AGENTS.md so every agent holds it.
