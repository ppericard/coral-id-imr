# What a duplicate photo is: a burst or the same framing; another zoom, framing, angle or pose is an `alt`, every subject

Date: 2026-11-24. PR: #1524. Rules or docs touched: judging/FISH.md "Candidates" `keep`; judging/SEASLUG.md
"Candidates: the judging files" `keep`; judging/BRIEF.md "Candidate pass" `keep` and "Per genus" `duplicates`. Study:
[docs/reviews/2026-11-23-what-a-duplicate-is.md](../reviews/2026-11-23-what-a-duplicate-is.md) (#1506).

## Context

Every photo of a multi-photo fish observation is a candidate (Q226), and each further photo is judged `best`, `alt` or
`dup` against the others of its observation. 8,593 of the 10,494 further photos of fish observations (82%) were judged
`dup` and stay out (Linear REE-252, #1504). The fish rubric's `alt` listed another side, phase, pose or light, its `dup`
a burst or the same framing, and it said nothing about another zoom. Pierre asked on 2026-11-23 21:31 what a duplicate
really is: «Si c'est une autre prise de vue plus serrée ou plus large, est-ce que c'est vraiment un doublon?» (Linear
REE-257). In a seeded sample of 100 sure fish `dup` extras, 32 were another zoom and 29 another angle, side or pose;
31 were a burst or the same framing (#1506).

## Decision

Pierre, 2026-11-24 06:45 Manila, questions session, group 2c: «ok» to the definition, for every subject. His word is
«ok»; the wording below is the integrator's, as put to him:

- `dup`: a burst or the same framing, with nothing new to see;
- `alt`: another zoom, framing, angle or pose of the same animal or colony.

It is written once, in the same words, in each subject's rubric (rule S1), worded by what it acts on (the photos of one
observation), not by subject. The examples the rubrics already gave for `alt` stay (another side, another phase, other
light; for corals another part of the colony, the colony shape, polyps out or retracted): applying the answer, not a
change. Nothing was added beyond his words: no rule on how good an `alt` must be and no limit on wide shots (#1506's
section 3 had more clauses; they were not put to him and are not applied).

**One declared difference, corals** (rule S1; its reason is rule P2): colony, close-up and corallites are separate views,
so a coral photo is judged `best`, `alt` or `dup` within its observation **and view**, and a zoom that moves it to
another view is judged within that view, not as an `alt` of the first. A coral `dup` also holds against a photo of the observation already
live or added (Pierre, 2026-11-03). The sea slugs follow the shared wording; their fetch takes one photo per
observation today, which is Linear REE-252's change, not a difference this record keeps.

## Consequences

- **Nothing is judged again here** (rule P7). The recorded `keep`s stay as they are until a pass Pierre approves. New
  judgements follow the definition at once.
- The P7 request for that pass is in Linear REE-257 and the PR: the sure fish `dup` extras in the species still short
  of easy adults (`tools/fetch_fish.py` `shortness`), per family, a scripted part from the recorded codes and an agent
  part, with their cost.
- A further photo judged `alt` holds half an observation's room against its species' cap (Q226, `obs_cost`), so a
  pass that turns `dup`s into `alt`s leaves less room for the refill in those species.
- Players see nothing until such a pass: the rubric only.
