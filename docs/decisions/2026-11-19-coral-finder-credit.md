# The Coral Finder book is a source like any other, with no credit line of its own

Date: 2026-11-19 (Manila). PR: #1194. Rules or docs touched: README "Credits and licences" (Photos, Text and data).

## Context

Since its crops left the app (2026-11-08), README's credits kept a line of its own for the Coral Finder 2022 book: no
photo of it is used, it stays a cited source, the Coral ID Key's boxes are our own drawings. In the app the book is
cited under References only (`tests/pages.smoke.js` `credits`).

## Decision

Pierre, 2026-11-19 08:18 Manila, on that line: "Je suis pas sur que c'est une bonne chose de mettre ça en avant.
Maintenant c'est juste une source comme les autres." The line goes; "Text and data" says the other books, guides and
papers behind a tip or a look-alike are cited where they are used.

## Consequences

The book keeps its citations where it is used (References, the tips and look-alikes that come from it); no credit or
licence text singles it out.
