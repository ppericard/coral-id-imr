# Keep the independent review method in the repository

Date: 2026-11-26. PR: #1622. Rules or docs touched: W8, AGENTS.md, integrator guide and review reference.

## Context

The shared brief and the 14 disciplinary mandates had been developed on the campaign issue. A future integrator
needed to be able to find and reuse them without carrying the whole method in its startup instructions.

## Decision

Pierre, 2026-11-26: « ça serait bien que ce travail qu'on vient de faire il soit aussi ailleurs que juste dans un issue »,
and « notamment au document de gouvernance des agents ». Keep the agreed review method in the repository, linked
from the agents’ on-demand reading and the existing independent-review rule.

## Consequences

The [review guide](../reference/independent-reviews.md) holds the reusable brief and mandates. Linear holds the
timing and state of each round. The coordinator can prepare a full round separately from dispatching its reviewers.
