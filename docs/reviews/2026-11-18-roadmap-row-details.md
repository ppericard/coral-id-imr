## Engine coherence review

The spec of the decision Q316 (Pierre, 2026-11-09) of the row "Engine coherence review: its 8 decisions".

- A componential difficulty model, an evolution of the current Elo engine: one difficulty per question = rank offset + family + genus + species + photo components, each shrunk toward its parent while it has few answers; the online update splits the surprise among the components by their uncertainty (the TrueSkill / simplified Kalman rule). With only genus + photo it must reproduce today's v31 ratings within rounding; the genus and species parts count at family rank too (Pierre: yes). Corals: no-regression parity with v31 in `tests/core.test.js`.
- Order: (1) the offline fit first, read-only; (2) the sheet's per-subject `?stats=1` with the shared chain; (3) the app reads the unit difficulty in place of `gd`. Rule L11 (level 2 at least; level 3 for any value chosen rather than fitted).
- Q136 (photos rated against the genus ability): the sheet rates each photo against one global level per player while the app picks and expects photos against the player's genus ability, a mismatch that can bias the ratings under adaptive selection (Bolsinova, Gergely & Brinkhuis 2025). #637 built it (sheet v67) and was closed too far behind; its branch `claude/photo-genus-ability` keeps it (a clone made before the 2026-11-08 rewrite never pushes it). ## Analyses multiple testing open items

Detail of the row "Analyses: what the multiple-testing correction leaves open" (statistical methods study of 2026-11-05, §5.3; the correction itself: spec/analyses-method.md, Multiple testing).

- (a) A spread (SD) near 0 reads clear because its interval is not valid at the boundary: test variance components by a likelihood-ratio test against a 50:50 mixture of χ²₀ and χ²₁ (Self & Liang 1987, Stram & Lee 1994) or a parametric bootstrap.
- (b) One decision milestone per question, or alpha-spending over the planned ones (Lan-DeMets), the earlier ones labelled interim.
- (c) Runtime: 999 refit draws cost about 1.7 minutes per 1,000 answers, so the Action's 2 hours run out near 55,000 answers: split the draws over cores (`worker_threads`) or use a wild cluster bootstrap (Cameron, Gelbach & Miller 2008).
- (d) "Answers needed" for a constant-within-player effect should say players needed.
- (e) PQL shrinks variance components: cross-check the primary effects once offline with a Laplace or Bayesian fit.
