# The batch refit of the engine's values, measured on the real log (Q351, 2026-11-14)

Read-only study for Q351's batch refit (ROADMAP "The expectation's slope, and per-format guess rates"; #651's choices,
decided by Pierre on 2026-11-14, Q351: "then a batch refit"), run now as decided by the integrator in the step-2 study
([2026-11-14-q351-step2.md](2026-11-14-q351-step2.md), "Decided by the integrator" (b)-(d)). It changes nothing in the
app or the sheet. The diagnosis's plan ([2026-11-06-overconfidence-diagnosis.md](2026-11-06-overconfidence-diagnosis.md)
§5.3): refit the photo and genus difficulties in batch with standard errors at each Analyses milestone, marginal or
penalised random effects (not joint maximum likelihood), and measure next-answer log-loss and the photo-residual slope
(0.26 in the diagnosis) on the answers after each refit. As decided for this run, fish are a declared data set and a
learning drift on today's Elo is a declared arm of its own, its grid reaching above η 0.1.

## Plan, declared before any fit or arm was run

Written and committed before any output of the fits or the arms below (rule L11). The only outputs seen before it are
step 2's replay checks, re-run on this copy (below, "Data"), which print no arm.

**Data.** The sync sheet's Answers tab, read-only through the Google Sheets connector on 2026-11-14 at about 21:55
Manila: 5,668 answers up to 2026-11-13 16:40 Manila, the same rows as step 2 (no answer since). The start records
(`reefid-sync-coral-start`, `reefid-sync-fish-start`) from the Progress data tab. Labels as in step 2 (P1-P14 by their
row in Progress data, A1 and A2 the two devices never signed in); the copies holding names stay outside the repository
(rule D2). Nothing is written to the sheet. Step 2's replay checks, re-run on this copy: BASE's final state equals
`core.js` `replayProgress` on all 432 coral and 266 fish records, and BASE's ability before an answer matches the logged
one within 0.015 on 803 of 1,255 coral rows with a scoring record (64%, as step 2). Corals: 3,308 scored answers; fish:
2,355 (surprise cards left out, as `core.js` `subjRows`).

**Two declared data sets, two families**: corals and fish, each with its own family of four tests under Holm at
α 0.05. The unit of inference is the player (rule L12).

**Milestones.** A refit at every 1,000 answers of the subject, the Analyses' cadence: corals at 1,000, 2,000 and
3,000 (2026-11-03, 11-05, 11-09), fish at 1,000 and 2,000 (11-07, 11-12). The answers after the first milestone are
the forecast answers: each is predicted with the values of the latest milestone before it, frozen until the next one.
Before the first milestone, every arm uses the logged inputs, so the arms differ only from it on.

**Two sets of values, each frozen at a milestone T**, so that the batch estimator is compared with today's estimators
on the same answers and the same staleness:

- **TODAY** (today's estimators run on the log before T): `core.js` `eloReplay` on every row before T, all subjects
  (the sheet's `stats_` rates every subject in one replay); each photo's difficulty as `model.js` `photoDiff` blends it,
  (n × rating + 5 × prior) / (n + 5), the prior its unit's offset, plus for a fish photo its judged ease's prior
  ((3 − ease) × 0.5, the ease from today's `fish-game.json`); the question's difficulty the mean over its photos, plus
  0.5 for a photo alone (`PICKD.SINGLE`); the memory offsets `eloReplay`'s at T, each answer's bucket from the player's
  own history with its photos (`memBucket`, `memFam`); the format offsets `core.js` `fmtOffsetsOf` on the rows before T.
- **REFIT**: the batch model below, fitted on the subject's answers before T of the trusted players (`core.js`
  `trustOf` on those rows, as today's photo ratings use). A photo not in the fit takes its unit's offset (and, for fish,
  its ease's part); a unit not in the fit, 0.

Both keep the mix-ups term as logged (scoring record; 0 without one), the recall of a comeback question as logged and
the guess rate 1/k.

**The batch model** (per subject; maximum a posteriori with the variances estimated, the penalised random-effects
fit the diagnosis recommends, never joint maximum likelihood):

P = g + (1 − g) · R · σ(η), η = a_p + u_(p,unit) + β1 · log(1 + m) + β2 · log(1 + n) − b_unit − mean over its photos of
r_photo − s · solo − mean over its photos of mem_bucket + o_format − c

- m the player's earlier answers on the unit, n on the subject: the person side moves over time through these two
  practice terms, so learning is not pushed into the photos met later; what they do not capture stays in a_p and u.
- Random effects: a_p ~ N(0, σa²), u ~ N(0, σu²), b ~ N(0, σb²), r ~ N(κ · (3 − ease), σr²) (κ for fish only, their
  judged ease; corals have none, κ 0). Fixed: β1, β2, s (a photo alone), the memory offsets of buckets 1-3 (bucket 0,
  a photo new to the player, the reference), one offset per format (weak prior N(0, 3²), so they carry the intercept);
  c the logged mix-ups term.
- The variances are estimated, not set: Laplace-EM (each σ² the mean of the squared posterior means plus their
  posterior variances, refitted until stable). The standard errors are the square roots of the inverse Hessian's
  diagonal at the mode, given the variances.
- The overall location is not identified between the players and the items, so every comparison with today's values
  uses centred values (correlations, slopes, spreads, the formats relative to 4 choices), never a mean shift.
- Unweighted: the person effects and the practice terms carry what today's weight n / (n + 10) does for beginners.

**Arms**, each replayed per player through today's scorer (`core.js` `scoreAnswer`, as step 2's BASE), the
abilities replayed against the arm's own inputs:

- **BASE**: today's rules with the logged inputs (step 2's BASE). A reference row, and the comparator of the drift.
- **TODAY**: the photo, unit, memory, single-photo and format values above, frozen at each milestone.
- **REFIT**: the same from the batch model.
- **DRIFT** (a learning drift on today's Elo, no variances): BASE, plus after each answer the ability of the unit
  shown rises by η (its record's `th`, clamped to `ELO.lo` / `ELO.hi` as `eloStep` does), misses included.
  η ∈ {0, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.6}, chosen by leave one player out (lowest total log-loss on the
  other players). 0 is in the grid, so a fold may choose no drift. If 0.6 is chosen in most folds, the optimum may lie
  above the grid: that is reported, and a wider grid is run as exploratory, outside the family.

**The family, per subject** (four tests, Holm at α 0.05):

1. REFIT against TODAY, next-answer log-loss on the forecast answers: the mean over players of each player's mean
   difference, exact two-sided sign-flip over the players with 20 or more forecast answers.
2. REFIT against TODAY, the photo-residual slope on the forecast answers: with ρ the arm's photo residual (the mean
   over the question's photos of the photo's difficulty minus its unit's part: TODAY n / (n + 5) × residual, REFIT
   r_photo), the fit P = g + (1 − g) · R · σ(a_format + b1 · (x + ρ) − b2 · ρ) on each arm's log-odds x; b2 is the
   slope of the photo residual (1: the residuals are on the right scale; under 1: too spread). The difference of the
   arms' b2, delete-one-player jackknife standard error (each replicate refits the batch model and recomputes TODAY
   without that player), t on players − 1 degrees of freedom.
3. DRIFT against BASE, next-answer log-loss on every scored answer, players with 20 or more (as step 2), sign-flip.
4. DRIFT against BASE, the calibration slope on answers whose unit had fewer than 10 earlier answers (step 2's
   measure), jackknife with the choice of η redone without the deleted player.

Expected by the diagnosis: REFIT lowers the log-loss and moves the photo-residual slope toward 1. Expected from step 2:
the drift lowers the log-loss a little. No equivalence margin: "not shown" means not shown, not shown absent.

**Descriptive, not tested** (with their answers and players):

- BASE's row beside the arms; the photo-residual slope under BASE (the residual today's sheet served at each answer,
  reconstructed as in the check below).
- The full-log refit against today's values at the end of the log: photos, units, format offsets (relative to 4
  choices), memory offsets, the single-photo term, and the abilities (the refit's a_p + u + practice at each player's
  last answer on a unit, against BASE's final ability): correlation, the slope of today's on the refit, the spreads,
  and how far today's photo and unit values sit from the refit in standard-error units (the share beyond 2).
- The variances and practice terms each fit estimates, by milestone.
- Guardrails read with their intervals: the mean predicted against the mean right per format with 100 or more
  forecast answers (REFIT's absolute gap no more than TODAY's + 0.02); the slope from 10 earlier answers under DRIFT
  (its 95% jackknife interval meets [0.8, 1.25]).

**Checks, before any arm** (they block if they fail):

- TODAY reconstructed online (each answer with `eloReplay`, `fmtOffsetsOf` and the memory on the rows strictly
  before it, nothing frozen) against the difficulty and memory the game logged, on the rows with a scoring record:
  the share within 0.05 and the median absolute gap, per subject. It blocks if the median gap of the difficulty is
  above 0.1 on either subject; the cause is then found before any arm is read. The device caches the sheet's
  statistics for up to 10 minutes and the admin's exclusions are not in the export, so close, not equal.
- The standard errors on simulated logs: 20 logs drawn from the full-log coral fit on the same rows (the outcome
  redrawn), each refitted; the coverage of the 95% intervals of the photo and unit difficulties and the correlation of
  the estimates with the values drawn from. This checks the errors under the model, not its misspecification.

**Exploratory, outside the families**: anything run after looking, labelled so.

**Limits known before running.**

- The same 5,668 answers step 2 looked at. The drift and fish leads came from them, so a "shown" here is not a fresh
  confirmation; the fresh test is the re-run at about 20 players (step 2's question (a)).
- At the 1,000-answer cadence there are only three coral refits and two fish refits; the first coral refit rests on
  1,000 answers of about 10 players.
- A replay keeps the questions that were asked: with other values the game would have chosen other photos and formats.
- Frozen values stand between refits; the deployed design would keep online steps between them (diagnosis §5.3), so
  both arms here are a lower bound on what each set of values gives online.
