## The Gibbs sampler (`gibbs.py`)

The same model and design as the Laplace fit; the SDs from their posterior (half-normal prior of scale 1 on each SD),
by data augmentation: what the player knew (the guess floor as a latent mixture), Pólya-Gamma weights, one exact joint
draw of every coefficient, each SD by interweaving (README "The Gibbs sampler"). The rules were committed and pushed
before any real run (051d66ad, 14:43 Manila). Revised in 04bf8a31 (15:44), after the synthetic runs and before any
real one: the real chains lengthened from 3,000 to 6,000 sweeps (the photo SD's slow mixing), the "own" truth of the
acceptance test given every fixed effect's median (it had taken the intercept and practice terms from the rated world),
the effective sample size recorded per replicate, and the prior check moved before the replicates. Acceptance: `fit.py`'s rule unchanged (20 replicates from the rated world's values and from the
sampler's own medians; mean of the medians within ±0.1, 15 of 20 covered). Plus: R-hat ≤ 1.1 and ESS ≥ 100 on the real
chains, and a median that moves by 0.1 or less under a half-normal prior of scale 0.5.

**Validated on simulated answers first** (`gibbs.py synth`; the table in the README): on the study's coral-like and
fish-like designs it recovers every SD the Laplace fit misses (the photo SD 0.48 and 0.54 for a truth of 0.46; τ 0.27
and 0.37 for 0.3; fish genus 0.26 and species 0.30 for 0.3), all passing; it fails the fish species rank offset (5% of
the answers). Its weak point: the photo SD's chains mix slowly (ESS 14-30 per 1,000 draws).

**One scale, two estimators** (exploratory, labelled; `refit/crosscheck.py`, 8 replicates, seed 5, not a declared
test): answers simulated on the real
coral design from the Gibbs medians, then fitted by the Laplace fit, give player 0.96, τ 0.58, genus 0.76, photo 0.47,
intercept 2.09, practice −0.35 and +0.47: close to what the Laplace fit gives on the real answers (0.98, 0.63, 0.83,
0.53, 1.90, −0.32, +0.43). So the two fits' gap is the Laplace fit shrinking every term on small clusters, the fixed
effects included (the logistic attenuation), not two different readings of the data: the Gibbs values, on a wider
scale throughout, are what the real coral answers support under the model and its prior.

**Reading.**
- **Corals: τ and the genus SD measured.** On the model's logit scale, a player's ability on one genus departs from

- **The coral photo SD is large, about 1.1-1.2 under both priors**, but moves by 0.11 between them where the declared

- **Fish: nothing measurable, even with the sampler.** The sampler recovers the fish-like design's SDs on simulated

**What moved, and why.** The coral τ and the coral photo SD are reached by the new estimator, not by the 1,042 answers:
per photo and per player × unit, the clusters barely grew (2.6 → 2.8 answers per coral photo, 1.3 → 1.25 per fish
photo: new answers mostly meet new photos). The fish lost their one measured value: the Laplace test did not finish
(its verdicts are missing, not negative), and the sampler, which is not biased by the small clusters, finds the fish
data too thin for every level under its prior.

**Should an engine value move?** Not now, and not by this study (choosing a value is level 3 of rule L11, Pierre's).
What the measured values hint about today's constants, read with care: today's steps written as prior variances
(#1373's 2B, the review's "today's rule as a special case": the photo step 0.8 as an SD of about 0.93, the unit step
`GENUS_K` 0.4 as about 0.66) live on the engine's own scale, which this model's scale need not match (the two
estimators above already differ by about 1.3 times):
- **Coral genus**: measured 0.83 (Laplace) to 0.94 (Gibbs), above today's 0.66 on either reading; with #1373's
  compression slope (today's per-unit offsets about 0.66 of the offline scale), it points the same way: today's genus
  offsets are narrower than the data. A hint for step 2's coral genus prior (a fitted value confirmed on held-out
  players, the literature review §7), not a value to set.
- **Coral photo**: about 1.1-1.2 (no value by the rule) against today's 0.93: the same order; no case for a change.
- **Q136 on the corals**: τ about 0.9 is a large per-genus spread of the players' ability, and 2C again finds the app's
  per-genus ability not centred (now in both subjects). If Q136's rating goes into step 2, the measured τ and the centred
  δ are its inputs (2C's rule), on the corals first.
- **Fish**: keep today's constants for every level below the family; nothing here supports a change.

**For the tool** (integrator's call, not done here): a rank offset with very few answers (the 4 species-rank answers)
could be left out of the fixed effects until it has, say, 30 answers, so that it no longer slows the Laplace fit past the
clock; and the fish refit "every 1,000 answers" will not reach the photo SD while answers mostly meet new photos.
